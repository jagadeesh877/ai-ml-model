Dialogues on the High Road
Two travelers met at a crossroad where four paths converged. One traveler carried a walking staff and a map of the province, while the other carried a leather bag of fresh fruit.
"Good morning, traveler," called the first traveler. "Which path leads to the ancient library near the river?"
"Take the road to the east," answered the second traveler with a smile. "It winds through the pine woods and crosses a stone bridge. If you walk at a steady pace, you will reach the library before the sun sets."
"Thank you kindly," replied the first traveler. "May your journey be pleasant and your path be clear."

The Curious Astronomer
On a clear autumn evening, an astronomer sat beneath the dark sky with a brass telescope. A young child approached and asked, "What are you looking at up there in the dark?"
The astronomer answered, "I am observing the planets and the distant constellations. See that bright yellow point of light? That is Jupiter, a massive world surrounded by orbiting moons."
"How far away is it?" inquired the child with wide eyes.
"Hundreds of millions of kilometers away," the astronomer explained. "Yet through this lens, we can see its rings and moons as clearly as if they were dancing in our own garden. When we study the night sky, we realize how vast the universe is, and how precious our small world remains."
The child gazed into the eyepiece and whispered, "The stars look like quiet lanterns across the sea."
